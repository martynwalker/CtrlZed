# Story Workshop — Beta 52

Choose **Review → Story Workshop…** to plan the current document. Select its workshop writing mode, normally Novel. The conversation begins with **How can I help you?** You can describe a character, an incomplete plot, a twist, or the difficulty you want help with. Opening the workshop and selecting a mode do not make an AI request.

Choose **Send** to continue through your configured direct AI provider. The assistant develops the plan and asks one or two useful questions at a time. It can work backwards from a twist, examine an assumption, suggest a clue or identify a missing motivation. It should distinguish an idea from an agreed fact and ask about uncertainties rather than silently settle them. You remain free to leave a section undecided or use a character who resists change.

The conversation and the structured notes are two views of the same planning work. **Characters** holds a cast list with editable, grouped notes. These cover traits and relationships; physiology, sociology and psychology; moral code, baseline behaviour, positive and negative triggers and signature tactics; dominant trait, redeeming quality, fatal flaw and voice rhythm. Each character also has the five arc stages: Want/Need/Ghost/Lie; disruption and resistance; escalating stakes, growth and midpoint; crisis and internal choice; proof of change and the new normal.

**Storyboard** provides an overview and editable story, event and clue records. The story plan includes premise, setting and tone, themes, twist, false premise, personal hook, opposition, ticking clock, dreams or memories, continuity, ending and the five main plot anchors. Event cards record what happens, its purpose, chronology, conflict, consequence, setup and payoff. The clue ledger separates its apparent first meaning from its true meaning, placement and eventual payoff.

Notes are marked **Proposed** or **Confirmed**. Direct edits are the author's decisions. AI interpretations and invented possibilities remain Proposed. A new fact extracted from an exact statement may be Confirmed; a conflicting AI change cannot replace an existing Confirmed value. Review the question or edit the note yourself. Confirm or remove individual suggestions as useful. The questions view keeps unresolved choices and continuity problems visible. For example, a switch from Jock to John should prompt a naming question rather than an assumed rename.

The workshop is editable without AI. Add, rename and remove cast members or cards, choose a group and field, then edit its value. Valid field edits update the document's planning notes as you type. **Undo planning edit** restores recent workshop changes separately from manuscript Undo; it also allows a whole AI turn to be undone. This short planning undo history is held in memory. Clearing the conversation retains the structured plan and questions, and is reversible through that planning undo history. Unsent conversation input is not a saved turn.

**Use in Character Voice** opens the chosen character's personality and voice notes in the existing rewrite preview. Those notes are editable there before rewriting. The bridge excludes the plot, twist and future arc outcomes so that a character is not automatically given knowledge of the ending. Long profiles identify their excerpts; the complete notes remain in the workshop. Change the underlying character in Story Workshop; temporary changes in the voice preview do not rewrite its stored profile. Applying a voice rewrite remains an explicit, separate manuscript action.

Workshop data belongs to this document and is saved inside **`.czd`** alongside the manuscript. Save the document to keep it; native password protection covers the planning data and conversation too. Existing recovery includes it. New documents, older files without workshop metadata and non-native documents open with an empty plan. Moving to another document closes the old workshop and cancels its request.

Ordinary Save uses `.czd` when there is planning data. Explicit Save As to DOCX, RTF or text is a body export: it explains that the plan is omitted, keeps the working document open and preserves its unsaved state and recovery. Password-protected documents retain their existing native-format restriction. The workshop is not part of manuscript text, printing, document fields, portable application profiles or body-only exports.

AI requests require AI language services to be enabled in Language settings and a configured direct ChatGPT, Claude or Gemini provider. Send shares the structured plan, a labelled window of recent workshop conversation and your new message. It does not read or send the manuscript body. Older conversation remains stored in the `.czd` file; the request states when earlier turns were omitted. The full structured plan is supplied, or the request stops with a size explanation rather than silently discarding planning notes. Closing, cancelling, changing provider/mode, editing planning notes or changing documents prevents a late response from being committed.

This first version is a planning conversation and reference board. It does not automatically write the novel, validate a live model's literary judgement, build a manuscript timeline or decide that every character must follow a positive growth arc. The bounded native record supports up to 64 characters, 160 events, 160 clues and 120 conversation turns, with 256,000 characters across the planning record. Individual fields hold up to 4,000 characters. Existing notes are not silently truncated when a limit is reached.
